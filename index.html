About

I solve business problems with AI — and ship them.

I'm an AI Developer who turns messy, high-stakes business problems into shipped, measurable AI products. Over the past 5+ years I've designed and deployed LLM applications, RAG pipelines, computer vision systems, and MLOps platforms for startups and enterprise teams.

I don't chase benchmarks for their own sake. I care about the numbers a business actually feels: latency, cost per request, accuracy on real data, and time-to-value. That's why my projects come with results attached — 70% less research time, $340k/yr saved, 98.4% defect detection.

My sweet spot is the gap between a promising model and a reliable product: evaluation harnesses, retrieval quality, cost control, observability, and the boring engineering that makes AI trustworthy in production.

When I'm not shipping, I'm reading papers, contributing to open source, and helping teams avoid the "demo that never becomes a product" trap.
